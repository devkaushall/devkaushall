Academics

Academics from Play Group to Class VIII

A structured curriculum based on the CBSE pattern, focused on concept clarity, language fluency and creative thinking.

Programmes

Three stages of growth

Pre-Primary
P.G. · N.C. · K.G.

A warm, caring start with activity-based learning.

  • Motor skills and fine-motor activities
  • Rhymes, storytelling and basic phonics
  • Numbers and early concept building
  • Fun, play-based classroom routines
Primary
Class I – V

Building strong foundations in core subjects.

  • English and Hindi
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Science
  • Computer studies and smart classes
  • Creative arts
Upper Primary
Class VI – VIII

Deeper understanding, practicals and readiness for the next stage.

  • Science and Mathematics
  • Social Studies
  • English conversation mastery
  • Computer lab practicals
  • Competitive readiness

Subject lists are taken from the school’s official academics page and prospectus. Detailed syllabus, timetable and textbook list to be provided by the school office.

Teaching approach

Learning that is clear, active and joyful

  • English-to-English conversation — regular speaking practice to build fluent communication.
  • Smart classes — audio-visual modules make complex topics easier to understand.
  • Computer education — practical digital skills from early grades, with lab practicals in upper classes.
  • Unit tests and assessment — regular unit tests; the 2nd Unit Test for the current term is scheduled for 12–17 October 2026.
  • Parent–teacher partnership — regular parent–teacher meetings and homework diary review.

Expectations from parents

As published on the school website (English summary)

  • Check the child’s homework regularly and comment in the diary.
  • Give special attention to studies, cleanliness and discipline.
  • Students must maintain 75% attendance.
  • Mobile phones, gadgets or valuables are not permitted on campus.
  • Attend Parent–Teacher Meetings regularly.

अभिभावकों से अपेक्षा है कि वे अपने बच्चों के गृहकार्य (Homework) को नियमित रूप से जांचें तथा डायरी पर टिप्पणी अवश्य करें। विद्यालय में विद्यार्थियों की 75% उपस्थिति होना अनिवार्य है।
