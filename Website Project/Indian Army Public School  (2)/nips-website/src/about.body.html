About New Indian Public School

Empowering minds, shaping tomorrow — a co-educational school in Tulsi Nagar, Etawah, offering Play Group to Class VIII on the CBSE pattern.

Who we are

A school built on values, English and learning

New Indian Public School, Etawah aims to combine strong academics with character building. Our teaching focuses on concept clarity, language fluency and creative thinking, so children learn to ask questions and explore ideas.

We provide English-to-English conversation practice, smart classes, computer education and a range of yoga, music, dance and sports activities for all-round development.
