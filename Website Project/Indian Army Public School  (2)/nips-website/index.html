Academics

A structured journey from Play Group to Class VIII

Based on the CBSE pattern, our curriculum builds strong foundations in language, numeracy and thinking skills, step by step.

Pre-Primary
P.G. · N.C. · K.G.

Activity-based learning, motor skills, rhymes, basic phonics, numbers and storytelling in a warm, caring environment.

Primary
Class I – V

CBSE-aligned English, Mathematics, Environmental Science, Hindi, computer studies, smart classes and creative arts.

Upper Primary
Class VI – VIII

Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, English conversation practice, computer lab practicals and competitive readiness.
