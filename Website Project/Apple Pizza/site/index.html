A strictly vegetarian kitchen

Every single item on the Apple's Pizza menu — all 60+ of them — is 100% vegetarian, and the brand markets itself as "100% pure veg". Look for the green veg mark on every card on this site: no exceptions, no fine print.

Our story

An Etawah original, baking since around 2016

What began as a single pizza counter at MKD Supermart, Friends Colony has grown into one of Etawah's most-loved veg pizza names — with a second outlet near KKDC 1st Gate, Pakka Talab and its own ordering app. The promise hasn't changed: hot, cheesy and fresh, at prices that make a ₹300 meal for two perfectly normal.
