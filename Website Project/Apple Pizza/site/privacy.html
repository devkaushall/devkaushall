Privacy Policy

Plain-language, honest, and short — because this website collects almost nothing.

Last reviewed: 10 October 2026.

What this website stores

  • Your cart and chosen outlet are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they survive page reloads. They never leave your device except when you deliberately send an order message (WhatsApp/email) or place an order on a third-party platform.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no payment details are collected here. This website does not process payments.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers are embedded in this website as shipped.

What happens when you contact the restaurant

If you call, WhatsApp, or email Apple's Pizza, the outlet receives the details you choose to share (name, phone, address, order). Those channels are governed by the respective providers and the outlet's own handling. The contact form on this site simply opens your own email app pre-filled; nothing is uploaded to a server by the website.

Third-party links

Links to Zomato, the official ordering platform, Google Maps and Facebook are provided for convenience; their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking cookies. Local storage (cart/outlet preference) is used as described above and can be cleared anytime from your browser settings.

Children

The website is a public menu and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.
