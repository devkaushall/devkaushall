Swad Ki Home Delivery

The honest story of Etawah's own pure-veg pizza house — told only with what we can verify.

Started at a supermarket. Stayed for the cheese pull.

Apple's Pizza opened its first counter at MKD Supermart, Friends Colony around 2016 — public listings show the brand in business for roughly a decade, and the Justdial listing dates to mid-2017. The idea was simple and stubbornly local: 100% pure-veg pizzas, loaded with mozzarella, priced so that two people can eat well for about ₹300.

That promise earned a loyal following — hundreds of public ratings across Justdial and other platforms — and a second outlet near KKDC 1st Gate, Pakka Talab (Ashok Nagar), listed since 2020 and today the higher-rated of the two.

What we stand for

  • Pure veg, no exceptions. Every item on the official menu is marked vegetarian — the brand advertises itself as "100% pure veg pizza".
  • Hot & fresh, honestly priced. The live menu runs a flat 40% off pizzas, and value packs (Pizza Mania) start at just ₹84 after discount.
  • Home delivery that's part of the name. The outlet's own wall banners say it best: "Swad Ki Home Delivery".

The journey so far

  • ~2016 — First pizza counter opens at MKD Supermart, Friends Colony, Etawah.
  • 2017 — Brand appears on public business listings (Justdial), quickly collecting local ratings.
  • 2020 — Second outlet opens near KKDC 1st Gate, Pakka Talab, Ashok Nagar.
  • Since then — Menu grows to 60+ veg items across four pizza tiers, Pizza Mania packs, garlic breads and sides; the brand appears on Zomato, magicpin and other delivery listings.
  • 2026 — The brand's own digital ordering platform (applespizza.com) is live with an Android app on Google Play; this website refreshes the brand online.

Behind the counter

The brand's official ordering platform is published under the name Anuj Kumar — Apple's Pizza. Day to day, it's a local team baking for a local crowd: students hanging out after class, families ordering a weekend blast, and regulars who know exactly which pepper combo they want.
