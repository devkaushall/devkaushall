Frequently asked questions

Short, factual answers. Where something isn't confirmed by the brand, we say so.

Is Apple's Pizza really 100% vegetarian?

Yes. The brand publicly states "100% pure veg pizza", and every item on its official ordering menu is marked vegetarian — including all pizzas, garlic breads, pockets and beverages.

Where are you located in Etawah?

Two outlets: (1) 1st Floor, MKD Supermart Mall, Friends Colony (near Indian Oil petrol pump), Etawah 206001; (2) Near KKDC 1st Gate, Pakka Talab, Main Road, Ashok Nagar, Etawah 206001. See the Outlets page for maps.

What are the opening hours?

Both outlets open at 10:00 AM daily. Closing shows as 9:00 PM on some listings and 10:00 PM on others — call +91 89380 09090 before a late-night order.

How do I place an order?

Four ways: build a cart on this site and send it by WhatsApp/call; order on the brand's official platform (applespizza.com) or its Android app; or order via Zomato where available. Phone: +91 89380 09090.

Do you deliver? What's the delivery area and fee?

Home delivery is offered by both outlets per their public listings. Coverage area, minimum order and delivery fee are set by the outlet and confirmed when you call — we don't publish numbers the brand hasn't.

What payment methods do you accept?

Not published by the brand online. Expect cash/UPI at outlet or on delivery (typical for local outlets); the official app and Zomato support their own online payments. Confirm on call.

Can I customise my pizza?

Yes — the official menu supports crust upgrades (Cheese Burst, Pan), extra/double-extra cheese and nine toppings (black olive, red paprika, jalapeño, paneer, mushroom, golden corn, onion, capsicum, tomato), priced per size. Use "Choose & add" on any pizza here.

What sizes are available?

Most pizzas come in 7" Regular, 10" Medium and 13" Large. Pizza Mania singles/doubles are 7" Regular; combos and sides have their own pack sizes.

Is the 40% off real? Any conditions?

The official menu currently lists pizzas with a 40% discount against MRP — our prices mirror it. The brand's "Weekend Pizza Blast" says "selected" pizzas, so treat the discount as menu-live and confirm at order time.

Do you have dine-in seating?

The Friends Colony outlet shows a small dine-in area in its public photos and Facebook lists dine-in; the Pakka Talab listing highlights takeaway and delivery. See Outlets.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Orders placed by phone/WhatsApp are handled directly by the outlet — call them immediately. Orders via Zomato or the official app follow those platforms' cancellation policies.

Do you take bulk or party orders?

The brand is also listed under caterers on Justdial, so bulk enquiries are reasonable — call +91 89380 09090 or email appizza7252@gmail.com to discuss.
