Three Learning Stages

One journey, step by step

1 PRE-PRIMARY

Play Group (P.G.)

  • Joyful first steps into school life — Vidhyaarambh celebrations and activity days.
  • Rhymes, stories, motor-skill play and early English listening & speaking.
  • Warm, attentive care in small groups.
2 PRIMARY

Classes I – V

  • Strong foundations in language, mathematics, EVS and computer basics.
  • Smart-class audio-visual lessons that make concepts click.
  • English-to-English conversation practice across the day.
3 MIDDLE

Classes VI – VIII

  • CBSE-pattern curriculum with structured unit tests and revision.
  • Practical computer-lab work and digital skills.
  • Confidence-building through presentations, yoga, music and dance.
Our Teaching Approach

How a NIPS classroom feels different

English-to-English Conversation

Children are encouraged to speak in English throughout the day, building the fluency and confidence that lasts a lifetime.

Smart-Class Learning

Interactive smart boards and audio-visual modules turn difficult chapters into easy, exciting discoveries.

Regular Assessment

Unit tests and continuous practice — like our scheduled 2nd Unit Test (12–17 Oct 2026) — keep every child on track.

Computer Literacy

Hands-on computer training from the early grades in a well-equipped lab, preparing children for a digital world.

Yoga, Music & Dance

Regular yoga, meditation, music and cultural dance sessions for physical and emotional well-being.

Individual Attention

A 20 : 1 student–teacher ratio means every child is known, heard and supported personally.
