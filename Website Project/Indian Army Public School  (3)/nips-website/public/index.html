Welcome to NIPS Etawah

A nurturing second home for your child in Etawah

New Indian Public School brings quality education on the CBSE pattern to Tulsi Nagar, Etawah — from a child’s first day in Play Group to Class VIII. We pair strong academics with confident English speaking, smart-class learning, yoga, music and dance, so each child develops fully and happily.

  • Strict English-to-English conversation that builds real speaking confidence.
  • Smart classes & computer labs for practical, visual learning.
  • Safe, CCTV-monitored campus with pick & drop transport.
