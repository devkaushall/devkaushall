Since our first batch — a growing family

Empowering Minds, Shaping Tomorrow

New Indian Public School is dedicated to the all-round development of every child — academically, physically and morally — on the CBSE pattern, from Play Group to Class VIII. Establishment year — confirm with school

OUR MISSION

Education, sport & a healthy life for every child

To empower young minds through education, sports participation and a healthy lifestyle — making opportunities accessible to school children of every background, and ensuring equal opportunities for all, including children with special abilities.

OUR VISION

A platform where children become leaders

  • School children reaching their potential through education and sport.
  • School events as a platform for future leaders and healthy citizens.
  • Values of support, mutual respect, team spirit and emotional stability.
Our Core Values

Values we live by, every single day

As our Principal says, education is “the most powerful weapon you can use to change the world.” Alongside academics, we work to instil values, life skills and habits that make our students stand out — collaboration, teamwork, critical thinking and emotional balance.

Respect Integrity Compassion Excellence Curiosity
