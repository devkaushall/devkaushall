Academics

CBSE-pattern classrooms from Play Group to Class VIII, with a public emphasis on spoken English and smart-class teaching.

01

Pre-primary · P.G., N.C., K.G.

Activity-based days: motor skills, rhymes, basic phonics, numbers and storytelling in a warm room. Vidhyaarambh for Play Group children is a published school event.

02

Primary · Class I to V

English, Mathematics, Environmental Science, Hindi, computer studies, smart classes and creative arts — described by the school as CBSE-aligned.

03

Upper primary · Class VI to VIII

Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, English conversation, computer-lab practicals and competitive readiness through Class VIII, the highest class currently advertised.

English-to-English conversation

A stated school priority: children practise English as the language of the classroom, not only as a subject period.

Smart classes

Audio-visual modules are used so that topics that feel abstract on a blackboard can be seen and heard.

Computer practicals

A computer lab is listed in the prospectus so that digital skills start in the lower grades.

Guidelines for parents

Published on the school’s academics page:

  • अभिभावकों से अपेक्षा है कि वे अपने बच्चों के गृहकार्य (Homework) को नियमित रूप से जांचें तथा डायरी पर टिप्पणी अवश्य करें।
  • बच्चे की पढ़ाई, साफ-सफाई एवं अनुशासन पर विशेष ध्यान दें।
  • विद्यालय में विद्यार्थियों की 75% उपस्थिति होना अनिवार्य है।
  • विद्यालय परिसर में मोबाइल फोन, गैजेट्स या कीमती वस्तुएं लाने की अनुमति नहीं है।
  • अभिभावक-शिक्षक बैठक (Parent-Teacher Meeting) में नियमित भाग लें।
