The school

Warm rooms, clear English, and a watchful campus in Etawah.

Parents looking for a neighbourhood English-medium school from Play Group through Class VIII will find a small, closely supervised setting here. The school publicly emphasises spoken English, smart-class teaching, computer practice, yoga and cultural work — not a long list of unverified awards.

Leadership is published on the official site: Principal Mrs. Jaybharti Kushwaha, Director Mr. Vikram Kushwaha, and Manager Mr. Radhey Shyam Shakya.

Academics

Three stages, one CBSE-pattern path.

Full academics
PG

Pre-primary

Play Group, Nursery (N.C.) and K.G. — activity-based days with phonics, numbers, rhymes, motor skills and storytelling.

I–V

Primary

English, Mathematics, EVS, Hindi, computer studies, smart classes and art — aligned to a CBSE-pattern classroom.

VI–VIII

Upper primary

Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, stronger English conversation and computer-lab practicals through Class VIII.
