How to apply

Talk to the office, then send this form.

  1. Call 8006000109 or 9720085999, or WhatsApp the same number.
  2. Share the child’s age and the class you want (P.G. to VIII).
  3. Visit Tulsi Nagar, Bharthana Road, with the child if the office asks for an interaction.
  4. Use the form so a written copy is stored on this site and, through your phone, on WhatsApp or email.
